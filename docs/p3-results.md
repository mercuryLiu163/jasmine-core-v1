## Bounds and limitations

- Native coverage is one macOS Codex CLI 0.159.3 installation/profile; no Desktop,
  other OS, cross-device P5 or full P4 Memory acceptance is claimed.
- The configured model profile is gpt-6.1-sol. Pinned tiktoken 0.14.0/o200k_base
  counts the exact rendered pack with `EXPLICIT_ENCODING_MODEL_UNVERIFIED`;
  it does not measure hidden platform prompt tokens or prove the model's encoding.
- Context is limited to 2500 selected-encoding tokens and 131072 UTF-8 bytes.
  Lossless nested Evidence reference encoding preserves all refs and values;
  Rule content and mandatory State are not truncated.
- Authorized controller bounds are 1800 seconds total, 140 seconds per native
  turn, at most 24 native turns/Raw inputs and eight extra refreshes; dynamic
  callbacks and machine Review preparation are bounded by 90 seconds. Unique
  approval publication is at most 90 seconds and live Guard approval also stays
  inside its original hook/native deadline. The reusable runner's limits are
  distinct from this actual validation controller's ledger.
- Independent semantic plans are not approval receipts. The prearmed executor
  performs one real Review CAS with current source/head/Truth/credential/lease
  checks; conflicts, expiry and uncertain results stop without retry or renewal.
- Cleanup reports native `cleanup=complete`, exit code 0, native PID/process group
  67121 and completed owned service PIDs 72520/67111, but explicitly reports
  `descendants_verified_gone=false`. No claim is made that every descendant was
  independently proven absent. Exact cleanup bytes/hash are indexed privately.
